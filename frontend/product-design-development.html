We begin with the people a product will serve. Together we clarify the need, the important journeys and a practical first version worth building.

  1. 01

    Problem & scope

    A shared definition of the users, needs, constraints and initial priorities.

  2. 02

    Customer journeys

    The steps people take to discover, use and get value from the product.

  3. 03

    Prototypes

    A way to review the experience and resolve important questions early.

  4. 04

    Initial product development

    The agreed first version, with the core workflows ready for use.

  5. 05

    Iteration & improvement

    Changes informed by customer feedback, operational needs and measurement.
