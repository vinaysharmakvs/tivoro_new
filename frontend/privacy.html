Privacy

How your details are handled.

Updated 19 October 2026 · Website preview

Project enquiries

When you send the contact form, Tivoro stores your name, email address, organisation if provided, selected service and project message in a private enquiry database. We use these details to understand the project, reply and manage the conversation. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your message.

Who can access an enquiry

The enquiry inbox is restricted to the authorised Site owner through sign-in and server-side access checks. Enquiries are not published on the website. If team email alerts are connected, a copy may also be sent to tivorosupport@gmail.com through the configured email service.

Security and spam protection

The form uses a short-lived, secure session cookie and a signed form token. Temporary, keyed hashes help limit repeated submissions; raw network addresses are not saved in the enquiry database. The security cookie lasts up to one hour and is not used for advertising.

Email drafts and copied briefs

If you choose “Open an email draft” or “Copy project brief”, that option prepares the details in your email app or clipboard. You decide whether to send them. Those options do not submit the project form to the enquiry database.

Keeping and removing information

Enquiries remain in the private inbox until Tivoro removes them. Archiving an enquiry keeps it in the inbox; it does not delete the details. Contact Tivoro to ask about your enquiry or request correction or removal where appropriate.

Analytics

This preview does not include advertising trackers or Google Analytics reporting. The Tools & Analytics page contains an illustrative planning example rather than client performance data.

Contact

For questions about your information, email tivorosupport@gmail.com.
